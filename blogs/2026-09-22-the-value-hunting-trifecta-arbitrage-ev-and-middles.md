The three public methods for finding mathematical edge in a sportsbook market: cross-book arbitrage (guaranteed profit), positive-EV (long-run edge against the sharp fair price), and middles (win both legs of a paired bet when a number lands in the window). What they share, where they differ, and which to chase.
